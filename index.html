Investigations

On-Site Diagnostics

State-of-the-art medical technology ensuring accurate, timely, and clinically reliable results without external referrals.

01
Pulmonary Function Test (PFT)
Measures lung volumes and airflow to detect obstructive and restrictive patterns.
02
Fractional Exhaled Nitric Oxide (FENO)
Non-invasive marker of eosinophilic airway inflammation to aid asthma diagnosis.
03
Full Night Polysomnography
Comprehensive sleep study recording brain activity, oxygen, and breathing patterns.
04
Overnight Oximetry
Ambulatory monitoring of blood oxygen saturation levels throughout the night.
